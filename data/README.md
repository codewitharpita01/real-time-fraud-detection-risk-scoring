# Fraud text dataset plan and bundled seed data

## What is included

`train.csv`, `validation.csv`, and `test.csv` are small, balanced, explicitly **synthetic** educational seed splits generated by `build_seed_dataset.py`. Each split contains 72 authored messages: 24 each for `scam`, `legitimate`, and `ambiguous`, across eight categories and English, Hinglish, and Hindi. Columns include `category`, `language`, `source_type`, and a unique `source_id`. There are no copied user messages or claims that any example is a real confirmed incident. The validation and test messages are different authored variants; the exact-text and source-ID leakage checks in the trainer enforce split separation.

Synthetic scam scenarios are grounded in publicly documented warning patterns, such as job fees and task deposits to unlock earnings. For example, the [FTC job and side-hustle guidance](https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2026/02/how-avoid-side-hustle-scam) describes upfront job payments, and its [task-scam guidance](https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2025/08/how-spot-avoid-task-scams) describes deposits to unlock tasks or withdrawals. India's [I4C/NCTAU advisory](https://cybercrime.gov.in/Webform/theme/resources/advisories/ADVISORY%20TAU-ADV-004_Captcha%20Filling%20Fraud_08.04.2025.pdf) describes fake task-job patterns. These sources support the scenario types only; they do not verify the synthetic messages or establish model accuracy.

## Suitable real datasets and their limits

- [Employment Scam Aegean Dataset (EMSCAD)](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/shivamb/real-or-fake-fake-jobposting-prediction) contains labeled job postings, but it is historical and job-specific. Its original collection and limitations are discussed in this [research paper](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00146-022-01469-0). It does not represent Indian scholarships, schemes, task scams, or current messages.
- [UCI SMS Spam Collection](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/228/sms+spam+collection) labels SMS as `spam` or `ham`, not confirmed fraud versus legitimate offers. Do not map every spam message to fraud.
- [UCI Phishing Websites](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/327/phishing+websites) is structured website-feature data, not raw SMS or job text. It would need a separate URL classifier and feature pipeline.

The repository does not bundle or silently relabel those external datasets. Obtain data under its license, preserve the original labels and source, document any label mapping, and keep records grouped by campaign/source across splits. Government and scholarship messages need source-based verification; wording alone is not a reliable ground-truth label.

## Training and evaluation

Run `python data/build_seed_dataset.py` to recreate the synthetic CSVs, then run `python scripts/train_model.py --data-dir data --output artifacts`. The trainer fits only on `train.csv`, tunes the risk thresholds on `validation.csv`, and reports text-classification and risk-level metrics on `test.csv`, including language/category reports, confusion matrices, false positives, and false negatives. Word and character features use the same Unicode normalization in training and inference.

The bundled benchmark is intentionally small and synthetic. Its metrics describe only these authored examples and must not be reported as real-world performance. In particular, the Hindi/Hinglish subset is synthetic and is not evidence of language-level performance on naturally occurring messages. Replace or supplement it with a legally usable, independently labeled, naturally occurring corpus before deployment or academic claims.
